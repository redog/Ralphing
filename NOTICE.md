# Third-party material

The root `LICENSE` applies to original work contributed to this repository.

Files under `research/archive/` are collected third-party web extracts retained
for research and provenance. Their original authors, source sites, and licenses
retain control of those works. Inclusion in this repository does not relicense
them under the repository license.

When adapting research into project documentation:

- write an original synthesis;
- link to the primary source;
- quote only what is necessary;
- preserve author and license information; and
- remove an archived extract if redistribution is not permitted or useful.
