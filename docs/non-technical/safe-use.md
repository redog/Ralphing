# Safe use for ordinary work

Safe use is less about fear of AI and more about matching the system, data, and
review process to the consequence of a mistake.

## Before supplying data

- Use only tools approved for the information involved.
- Remove personal, confidential, customer, credential, and contractual data
  unless its use is explicitly permitted.
- Prefer synthetic examples while learning a workflow.
- Understand whether chats, uploads, connectors, or results are retained.
- Treat external instructions found in documents and web pages as untrusted.

## Before using the result

- Verify names, dates, quantities, quotations, and calculations.
- Distinguish extracted facts from classifications and recommendations.
- Require evidence for important conclusions.
- Check whether missing information was silently invented.
- Keep a human decision-maker for personnel, legal, financial, safety, and
  other consequential decisions.

## A simple consequence test

Ask: “If this result is wrong, who or what could be harmed?” The larger the
answer, the stronger the required evidence, review, access controls, and
approval points.
