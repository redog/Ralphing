# Prompt brief

## Real-world goal

What should become easier, clearer, or complete after this work?

## User and decision

Who will use the result, and what will they do with it?

## Approved evidence

List the documents, data, or facts the model may use.

## Constraints

- What must be preserved?
- What must not be inferred?
- What information is sensitive?
- What is out of scope?

## Output contract

Specify the structure, length, tone, fields, ordering, or file format.

## Verification

Describe the source checks, calculations, acceptance criteria, or human review.

## Uncertainty behavior

State how missing, conflicting, or ambiguous information should be reported.

## Iteration notes

Record what changed between attempts and why.
